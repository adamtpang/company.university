# MASTERPLAN — Company University

The platonic ideal. What this project IS, at its best, independent of today's state.
Rarely changes. When it does, edit here and let NORTH_STAR.md's progress ladder and
EVIDENCE.md's numbers catch up to it, not the other way around.

## The thesis

Every top company gets a school. Build the real job, in person, watched. That is
the one hiring signal AI cannot fake.

## The reframe

Not a course seller. A hiring funnel that monetizes trust. Companies are flooded
with AI written applications and have no reliable way to separate signal from
noise. "We watched it get built" is the product.

## The mechanism: the JD to hire engine

1. Pick a company with real, verified hiring demand.
2. Reverse engineer their actual open job postings into a 6 week curriculum.
3. Co design the rubric and the unseen build brief with their HR. They commit
   final round interview slots before the cohort starts.
4. Gate entry hard. Admission is itself the first proof of skill, and it is
   public, not hidden.
5. Run the cohort to one real, shippable artifact mapped 1 to 1 to the job.
6. Certify via a one to three day OBSERVED, in person build at Network School.
   Fresh brief, seeded bugs, a live oral defense. This is the moat: an LLM can
   write the code, it cannot defend it live under real time questioning in the
   room.
7. Fast track passers into the company's final round. Never a placement
   guarantee. Placement guarantee language is what drew Newton School's FIRs
   and the CFPB action against BloomTech's ISA.
8. Publish honest, retention weighted outcomes. Feed them back into the next
   cohort's brief and rubric.

## The model: UNRESOLVED, decide before real outreach

Two incompatible pricing models are currently live in this repo. Only one can
be true.

- **A, shipped:** employer pays a flat fee or sponsorship per cohort.
  `/pilot` is $60,000, all in, one cohort.
- **B, the wider constitution (ECOSYSTEM.md):** company pays ONLY on
  placement, 15 to 25 percent of first year salary, with a clawback guarantee
  period. The student never pays, ever, under any model.

`/getin` currently charges the builder a flat $2,500 success fee once hired,
which directly violates model B's "student never pays, ever" rule. See
NORTH_STAR.md's flagged pricing conflict. Do not quote a price to a real
company or a real builder until this is picked.

## The wider ecosystem: the Talent Trifecta

Company University is one of three coordinated repos, sharing one constitution
(`ECOSYSTEM.md`, identical in all three).

- **company.university, the academy.** Raises a builder's ceiling, on track
  for a named company. Trains and certifies. Never scores.
- **darktalent.tech, the scout and stats engine.** Scores kinetic, shipped
  output today, and potential, learning velocity and the ETA to close a
  named gap. Never brokers.
- **skill.supply, the transfer market.** Matches, negotiates the fee, closes
  the placement. Owns the deal end to end.

Never sell to Network School members directly. They are supply, not the
customer. Revenue comes from companies.

## The discipline: standing rules, do not relitigate every session

- **Dream big, enter narrow.** The end state is every company on the
  `/board` leaderboard running a school. The next action is always the same:
  close ONE employer student hire loop, end to end, before building more
  infrastructure or scaling to a second company.
- **Real work is the only curriculum.** No toy projects, no simulations.
- **The company pays, in whichever model wins.** Never the student, never
  anything shaped like an income share agreement.
- **Never promise placement.** Always fast track to final round.
- **Publish only verifiable, retention weighted outcomes.** Never inflate a
  number that cannot be defended. See Tetr's 0.12 percent versus 2.6 percent
  credibility collapse, and BloomTech's CFPB action.
- **No em dashes anywhere.** Code, copy, docs, model output. Commas, colons,
  periods, or middle dots instead.
- **Proof over pedigree**, always, in how a builder or a company is
  described.

## Not: the scope guard

- Not a marketplace matching many students to many companies at once. One
  school is one employer's funnel, until Stage 1 is proven.
- Not a $200,000 luxury degree. That is Tetr. Win from underneath it: free
  or cheap, and a real job, not a credential and a hope.
- Not a bootcamp with a generic curriculum hoping a job exists at the end.
  That is what App Academy was for the founder personally, and it is the
  exact failure this model exists to fix: training tied to nothing, then a
  cold resume pile.
- Not a scoring or matching product. That is darktalent.tech and
  skill.supply's job, not this repo's.
